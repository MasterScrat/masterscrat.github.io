Flatland investigates the use of machine learning, specifically deep reinforcement learning, for railway planning. I was the project's lead researcher at AIcrowd, working in partnership with the national railway companies of Switzerland, Germany and France (SBB, Deutsche Bahn, SNCF).

I co-organized the associated Flatland 2020 NeurIPS Challenge, and ran a workshop on it at TheWebConference 2021: "Flatland: Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning on Trains".
